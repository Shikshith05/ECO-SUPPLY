Fleet Optimizer

Capacitated vehicle routing from a depot

Every vehicle starts and ends at the depot and carries at most its capacity. Results are compared with greedy nearest-neighbour routes and with the stops in the order received, on the same distance matrix.

Demand defaults to 1. Points outside the cached Bangalore road graph switch the request to straight-line × detour-factor distances.
Capacity "auto" = ceil(total demand / vehicles).
